Conference Guidelines

01. Formal Attire

All delegates are required to wear Western business attire during formal sessions.

02. Diplomatic Courtesy

Delegates must maintain respectful discourse at all times. Personal attacks or undiplomatic conduct are not permitted.

03. Language Protocol

The official working language of the conference is English. Formal diplomatic language is expected.

04. Attendance Policy

Attendance is mandatory for scheduled committee sessions and conference activities.

Qadiyya MUN x SMCP MUN

A Mystery-Driven Model United Nations Experience

Qadiyya MUN 2026 is designed as a student-driven Model United Nations conference where diplomacy meets investigation. Delegates are encouraged to think critically, solve complex global cases, negotiate under pressure, and grow as confident speakers, leaders, and problem-solvers.

Built around creativity, leadership, and meaningful debate, Qadiyya MUN offers a unique conference atmosphere for both first-time delegates and experienced MUNers. Through this collaboration, SMCP MUN supports a shared vision of youth diplomacy, learning, and impactful conference experiences.

Dates: 11–13 December 2026
