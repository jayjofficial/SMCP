Conference Guidelines

01. Formal Attire

All delegates are required to wear Western business attire during formal sessions.

02. Diplomatic Courtesy

Delegates must maintain respectful discourse at all times. Personal attacks or undiplomatic conduct are not permitted.

03. Language Protocol

The official working language of the conference is English. Formal diplomatic language is expected.

04. Attendance Policy

Attendance is mandatory for scheduled committee sessions and conference activities.
